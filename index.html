    ⚖️ Number balance

    Shape how each generated row is distributed.

    Zones of ten: 1–10, 11–20, … A minimum above 0 forces every zone to be represented.

    E.g. 3: a row like 3, 7, 11, 19, … (four odd numbers in a row) is rejected.

    🛡️ Popular anti-patterns

    Avoid the “obvious” combinations a lot of players pick — so a win is less likely to be shared.

    E.g. 3 rejects 5, 10, 15, 20 — four numbers spaced evenly apart, anywhere in the row.

    E.g. 3 rejects 4 numbers like 7, 17, 27, 37.

    E.g. 4 rejects 5 multiples of 7 like 7, 14, 21, 28, 35.

    Avoid all-low or all-high rows by requiring numbers from both halves of the range.

    Require at least one number above 31, since many players stick to calendar dates — fewer co-winners if you win.

    ✨ Smart weighting

    Bias the random picks toward certain numbers. Needs loaded history.

    The longer a number has gone without being drawn (within your lookback window), the higher its chance of being suggested. Purely a style preference — the real odds never change.

    Numbers drawn least often in the chosen period get a higher chance of being suggested.

    Drag left to make numbers from the lower half of the range more likely, right for the higher half. It only changes how likely each row is — every row stays possible and the odds of any row do not change.
